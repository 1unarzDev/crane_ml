## Generated in-repo (no third-party content)

- Tree, branch, root, log, rock, fern, shrub, litter and mannequin meshes: `Editor/TreeGen.cs`, `Props.cs`, `Humanoid.cs`, `ForestGeometry.cs`.
- Foliage alpha atlases (broadleaf, needles, fern frond) and fabric weave: `Editor/TexGen.cs`, written to `Generated/seed_<N>/Tex/`.
- Sky, fog, post-processing: HDRP volume profiles in `Profiles/`.
- Everything under `Generated/` is reproducible from `Specs/*.asset` or JSON plus the seed and may be deleted and rebuilt.
