# Navigation pilot: conventional-baseline failure map (DEV layouts)

_Generated by `Tools/NavPilot/sweep.py report`. Evidence label: IMPLEMENTED pilot (2.5D point mass, synthetic sensors, synthetic forest; not LIVE_SIM_VALIDATED, not PHYSICALLY_CALIBRATED). DEV seeds 0-5, which splits.json reserves for iteration: these numbers locate failures, they are not reportable results._

**Setup.** 864 episodes = 6 whole DEV layouts x 144 cells (branch density ['none', 'low', 'mid', 'high'] x speed limit [3.0, 5.0, 7.0, 10.0] m/s x latency tail ['nominal', 'heavy', 'severe'] x sensor ['stereo', 'lidar', 'fusion']). Stem density `medium` (800 stems/ha), 60 m corridor, wind `light_breeze`, 6 person targets per layout. Same layout seed = same run seed across cells (common random numbers). Baseline: log-odds 2.5D map, inflation r_body + 0.10 m, 48 headings x 5 speeds forward-simulated through the tracking loop, stopping rule d_free >= v tau + v^2/2a + 0.5 m with tau = 0.10 s and a = 6.6 m/s^2 (0.8 x tilt-limited), unknown = blocked.

Compute-time tails per planner cycle (period 50 ms): `nominal` p50 25 / p95 38 / p99 47 / p99.9 77 ms; `heavy` p50 31 / p95 66 / p99 160 / p99.9 521 ms; `severe` p50 37 / p95 211 / p99 559 / p99.9 2490 ms.

Throughput: 0.75 episodes/s per process (1151.0 s total wall in episodes); belief stopping-rule violations: 0.

Overall: success 28%, collision 62%, stuck 10%. Causes: latency_stall 232, tracking_lag 216, detection_miss 111, local_minimum 85, map_lag 39, thin_branch_miss 34, occlusion 12.

## 7. Reading the map (pilot interpretation; n = 6 layouts per cell, so single cells are +-20 points)

**Where the baseline holds:** no thin branches, nominal latency: 83-100% success at every speed up to 10 m/s for all
three sensors. Stems alone are not the problem at 800 stems/ha.

**Where it breaks:**
1. **Latency/stall tail (S, 232 collisions).** Moving from `nominal` (p99 47 ms) to `severe` (p99 0.56 s) drops even branch-free
   success to 17-67%, and at 3 m/s it turns stuck runs into crashes (3 m/s: collision 17% -> 68%). The rule uses
   tau = 0.10 s (nominal belief), so every stall longer than ~2 periods flies blind on the last setpoint. Points to
   **latency-aware speed selection** (tau from the p99/p99.9 age, plus a stall watchdog that brakes).
2. **Plan-execution gap (K tracking_lag, 216).** The obstacle was in the map in time and still there at contact, with no
   stall. Collision share at nominal latency grows with speed (3 m/s 17%, 5 m/s 54%, 7 m/s 67%). The rule's tau omits the
   0.3 s velocity-loop lag and the jerk ramp. Inflation (r_body + 0.10 m) is thin against the 0.1 m cells, 7.5 deg headings
   and gusts. Most K contacts are branches (164/216) brushed sideways while weaving. This also points to
   **latency-aware speed selection** (tau_eff = pipeline + tracking lag), or to a bounded residual on the clearance/speed.
3. **Thin-branch perception (T thin_branch_miss 34 + M map_lag 39 + O occlusion 12, all on ~2.4 cm branches, at the highest
   contact speeds, 6.4-7.4 m/s).** The branch was missed by the sensor, or seen but not yet occupied (the log-odds cell was
   pre-carved free at range, so it needs >= 2 hits), or eroded by free-space carving. Points to **foliage-aware obstacle
   sensing** (hit-protected / thin-structure-aware mapping, range-dependent detection priors, LiDAR+stereo union).
   Its weight is understated here because K and S often fire first in branchy cells.
4. **Local minima (L, 85) at 3 m/s in mid/high branch density.** The unknown-as-blocked map plus soft twigs mapped as
   obstacles turns the slab into a maze. The 2.5D vehicle also cannot climb over or duck under. Partly an artefact of 2.5D.
5. **Detection (111 runs whose only failure was detection_miss).** Recall falls 0.81 -> 0.64 from 3 to 10 m/s. The dominant
   miss is `blur_or_size` (207 of 261 at 10 m/s), then `never_in_fov` (the route never pointed the 66 deg RGB at the
   target). Points to **detection-aware search** (speed and heading chosen for detection, i.e. slow-down or look-at near
   likely target areas).

**Sensor comparison.** Fusion is not better than LiDAR alone in branchy cells. LiDAR at 10 Hz makes the 120 ms age gate
fail structurally (36% of cycles vs 22-24% for stereo/fusion), but its 360 deg map avoids stereo's FOV-driven occlusion and
local-minimum cases. Stereo collides most in `mid` (88%).

## 8. Caveats (what would overturn this)
- 2.5D slab and point mass: no climbing, no attitude dynamics, no LIO drift (state estimate = truth). Real drift would add
  a map-misregistration failure mode that is absent here.
- `margin-viol ticks` uses all circles, including soft (< 2 cm) twigs, so it overstates violations in branchy cells.
  Collisions use hard colliders only (TREEGEN_DESIGN: d >= 2 cm in the 1-4 m band).
- Map constants (l_miss -0.1 per column, clamp -1) approximate a 3D voxel map projected over the slab. With OctoMap's
  -0.4 applied per column, every branch density saturated at 0% success, so the T/M split is sensitive to this choice.
- Forest geometry is a seeded prior, not TreeGen output or survey data. Branch counts/diameters are [U].
- Cause labels use rule-based attribution with t_need from the belief stopping distance. "tracking_lag" is an extra label
  that the task's list lacked: mapped in time, no stall, still hit.
