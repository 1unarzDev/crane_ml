## Verdicts (written by hand after the runs; the tables above are generated by `mechanism2 --report`)

- Key controls replicate on fresh seeds: HYBRID loses 0.045-0.049 vs IMPLICIT (8/8 instances negative) with 5.2-5.8 collisions/ep; HYBRID_P and PH_orc are statistically indistinguishable from IMPLICIT; NP_nom ~ HYBRID, NP_none worse.
- Placebo (a) priority swap: inert as first written (SWAP, SWAP_F == HYBRID exactly), because the inherited lease tie rule gives agent 0 a stale virtual look, so the predictor never fires. With a stale-lease override (SWAP_V) it recovers about half of the effect (2.7 coll/ep, dP -0.014 to -0.022). Role asymmetry is not inert, and the swap is not symmetric to HYBRID_P.
- Placebo (b) random partner: RND_A (uniform) leaves collisions at 5.0-5.5 and dP at HYBRID level; RND_T5 (uniform over top-5) gives an intermediate 3.7-3.8. Prediction content matters.
- Placebo (c) symmetric: SYM == HYBRID_P (agent 0 never fires). SYM_V (both fire over stale leases) is worse than HYBRID_P at lambda=3 (dP -0.096, below HYBRID): two mutual avoiders can dodge each other into a different collision/gap.
- Belief divergence by dropping records: collisions fall only 6-18% (5.18 to 4.79 at 75% drop, lambda=1) and detection does not improve. Weak support for "homogenized beliefs" and not a clean test, because a dropped record falls back to the shared nominal plan.
- Dose-response: collisions fall with delay (5.18, 1.90, 1.11 at d=1,2,3) and travel 1 (1.19). HYBRID_P's gain shrinks at d>=2 (dP -0.024, -0.028 vs HYBRID -0.040, -0.045) while PH_orc removes collisions entirely but at d>=2 does NOT recover detection (-0.040, -0.044).
