# cad_iter3 critique (final: detailed bodies, interference-free, prop-clear)

Changes from iter 2: black motor bells, kit battery straps, camera-cage tabs, gold spacers under Cube/Jetson, header pins,
USB-Eth cable, battery lead boot moved to the rear face, power leads stop on the pack top, exact mesh interference test
with a documented allow-list (bolted/through-hole joints), render framing fixed.
Numbers: mass 1.843 kg, CoM (-3.0, 0.0, +17.3) mm, interferences 0, prop-disc clearance min +4.2 mm.

Remaining differences from the concept (accepted, listed so the user can judge)
1. Payload deck sits above the prop plane (z = 48 mm) so the model is taller than the concept; this is required for the
   Jetson/L2/radio not to cut the 7 in prop discs (see SPEC.md section 4). The concept is an AI render, not buildable at
   one level.
2. Cube Orange sits at the front centre of the upper plate, not off-centre: the free rhombus between the front props is
   only about 45 mm wide at x = 50..72 mm.
3. L2 is square-base + cylinder (envelope exact, shape approximate). Plates have no honeycomb cut-outs. No wiring runs
   along the arms (motor leads are inside the 46.6 g motor mass). No Tattu artwork. Gold bracket is a plain U, not the
   concept's rounded cage.
4. Antennas leave the radio at 12-14 deg tilt; positions of the real SMA mounts are unknown (ESTIMATE).
5. CoM x is -3 mm because the battery cannot slide further forward than the OAK-D bracket; this is reported, not hidden.
