## Track 1: [workshop] coordination paper ("[paper title withheld], except for a health beacon")
Source: `~/worktrees/crane-forest-sar/Tools/ForestSearch/` (paper in `paper/main.tex`; latest ae053c61, pushed).

**Closest competing results**
- Boutilier (IJCAI 1999): identical joint policies need a convention, and priority order fixes it. It covers our stale-plan collision mechanism in general form (NOVELTY2.md, claim A: PARTIALLY KNOWN).
- Becker, Carlin, Lesser & Zilberstein (2009); Unhelkar & Shah, ConTaCT: when communication pays, analysed by policy.
- Boldrer et al. (arXiv 2603.08379, 2026): communication-free multi-UAV coordination in real forests. It supports "silence can suffice" from the field side.

**Strongest claim the evidence supports**
1. *Theorem:* VoC = 0 exactly when looks return only NULL/DETECT (exact_dec.md).
2. *Held-out, N = 2, greedy:*
   - The oracle beats silence by at most 0.014 (never significant), while dropping the shared plan costs 0.06–0.15.
   - A 12-byte health frame recovers essentially all of full messaging's gain under permanent agent loss, at about 1% of the bytes.
3. *Held-out scaling* (`results/nscale_heldout.md`, preregistered): **the N = 2 beacon result does not generalise.**
   - HEALTH − IMPLICIT shrinks with N and turns significantly negative at N = 10 (−0.03 to −0.075).
   - The priority-order fix also degrades with N.
   - What does generalise: the shared default plan matters more as teams grow, roughly N^0.5–0.8, and mixing fresh messages with a stale plan is a hazard that grows with N.

   The paper's scope ("two searchers") is therefore the correct, defensible scope.

**Experiment most likely to overturn it**
- **Non-greedy planners.** The same-planner lookahead already shows VoC of +3.1 pp. A stronger planner, or a learned communication gate, could make communication pay in healthy teams too. That test is now direction D1, the learned gate supervised by coordination regret, in `Docs/DroneVisuals/frontier/`. It is DEV-only; the held-out prereg is draft only.
- **Second risk: imperfect self-diagnosis.** The beacon assumes the agent detects its own permanent failure. A sweep of missed and false self-diagnosis rates would show how fast the beacon's advantage decays. It is listed in RESPONSE_LOG as an open objection.

## Track 2: CRANE forest navigation study (NavPilot 2.5D, then the CRANE .NET harness, then Unity)
Sources:
- `Tools/NavPilot/results/E3_heldout.md` (written 11-10 09:2x; 800 held-out episodes, PREREG_SPEED.md frozen)
- `results/E2_heldout.md`
- `Docs/DroneVisuals/AUTONOMY_STATUS.md`

**Closest competing results**
- SUPER (Ren et al., Science Robotics 2025): safe high-speed flight in clutter with a backup trajectory, at about 20 m/s on real hardware.
- Falanga et al. (RA-L 2019): how perception latency limits safe speed.
- Zhang et al. (Nature Machine Intelligence 2025): learned high-speed flight.

All of these use real compute or tight latency. None measures how far max-safe-speed drops under a *heavy compute-latency tail* in a forest, nor how much speed each mitigation recovers.

**Strongest claim the evidence supports**
1. Under the heavy latency tail (2.5D, held-out, pooled over four disturbance families), **every stack's max safe speed v* at P(collision) ≤ 5% sits at 2.8–3.7 m/s.** At 7 m/s, E2 showed collision rates of 41–75%.
2. `pas_full` gives the only significant held-out gain: achieved progress +0.78 m/s [+0.07, +1.37]. Its v* gain of +0.72 has a CI that includes 0. Its biggest effect is in the drift family (v* +1.50, significant).
3. In the 3D CRANE .NET harness, 0 collisions at ≤ 4 m/s (48 flights; 49 in Unity). Collisions at ≥ 5 m/s are all brake_infeasible.

The two simulators agree on a speed ceiling of about 4 m/s. That agreement is qualitative; it has not been tested as paired rankings.

**Experiment most likely to overturn it**
1. **Counterfactual attribution** (`run_pending.sh` stage 1, the cf_fastperc / nodrift / nodrop replays, queued by the ops agent, plus the D3 DEV diagnosis). If removing the latency tail does *not* remove most 7 m/s collisions, the "compute tail × braking envelope" explanation is wrong. The study would then be about occlusion-limited free space instead.
2. **Ranking transfer, NavPilot → CRANE .NET** (direction D4; paired twin runs queued as `run_pending.sh` stage 2 plus 3). If Kendall τ between stack rankings is low at 5–8 m/s, the 2.5D held-out ranking (`pas_full` first) does not carry into 3D, and E3's conclusions shrink to "2.5D-only".
